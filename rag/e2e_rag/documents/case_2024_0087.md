## Nature of Complaint

The customer disputes the early termination fee applied to their contract. They claim a sales agent verbally communicated a fee of 1%, whereas the contract specifies 3%.

## Internal Findings

- The assigned sales agent has confirmed in writing that the correct 3% fee was communicated to the customer.
- The customer's signature appears on page 4 of the contract, which contains the fee schedule.
- Legal has been notified and is reviewing the case.

## Actions Taken

- Case logged in CRM under the customer's profile
- Legal team notified on date of receipt
- No financial offer or adjustment is to be made pending legal team response

## Next Steps

Await legal team assessment. No further communication with the customer should be initiated until legal provides guidance.
