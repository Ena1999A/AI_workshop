## Nature of Complaint

The customer disputes a vehicle repossession, claiming they were fewer than 90 days overdue on payment at the time of repossession. They also state they did not receive adequate advance notice.

## Initial Finding (First Review)

Internal payment records confirmed the customer was 91 days overdue on the March 2024 installment. Two written notices were recorded as sent to the customer's registered email address. Repossession was deemed procedurally correct.

## Revised Finding (Second Review — requested by customer's legal counsel)

A second review found that:

1. The first written warning was sent to an **outdated email address** that the customer was no longer using.
2. The second warning was sent **only 3 days before repossession**, which does not meet the adequate notice standard under Croatian consumer protection law.

As a result, the repossession procedure may not be legally defensible.

## Legal Team Recommendation

The legal team recommends initiating a **settlement offer** to the customer. No further repossession-related action should be taken without legal sign-off.

## Current Status

Case is open and under active legal review. The original case closure note ("no refund or compensation will be issued") has been **superseded by the second review** and should be disregarded.
