## Permitted Geographic Area

The leased vehicle may be used within the **European Union** and the **Western Balkans region** (Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania).

Use outside these regions requires **prior written approval** from the leasing department. Damage incurred outside the approved area is not covered by AutoLease partner insurance policies, and the full repair cost falls to the customer.

## Prohibited Uses

The vehicle may not be used for:

- Commercial transportation of passengers or goods
- Motorsport or track events
- Driving instruction (commercial or otherwise)
- Any activity that reclassifies the vehicle as a commercial-use asset

These activities change the vehicle's insurance category and may invalidate both the insurance policy and the leasing contract.

## Modifications

Vehicle modifications are **strictly prohibited** without prior written approval from the leasing department. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Window tinting or wrapping
- Aftermarket parts or accessories
- Mechanical or engine modifications

## Insurance Requirements

The vehicle must be insured under **both** of the following policies at all times:

- Comprehensive (kasko) insurance — without excess, or with an excess of up to €500 with AutoLease written consent
- Compulsory third-party liability insurance

The insurer must be on the AutoLease d.o.o. approved partners list, available on the customer portal. The insurer may be changed once per year with **30 days' written notice**.

If the customer fails to renew their insurance policy on time, AutoLease reserves the right to arrange insurance independently and pass the cost on to the customer.

## Accident Reporting

If the vehicle is involved in an accident, the customer must notify AutoLease **within 48 hours**. Failure to do so may result in financial penalties or contract termination.

## Liability at Handover

AutoLease d.o.o. is liable for any undisclosed defects present in the vehicle at the time of handover, as documented in the vehicle condition report. The customer assumes full responsibility for the vehicle from the moment of handover. AutoLease does not accept liability for mechanical failures arising after handover, unless attributable to a formally acknowledged manufacturing defect.
