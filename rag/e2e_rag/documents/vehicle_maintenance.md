## Customer Responsibility

The customer is responsible for all maintenance costs for the duration of the leasing contract. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Regular servicing
- Tyres and brakes
- Oil and other consumables

## Servicing Requirements

- Servicing must follow the **manufacturer's recommended intervals**
- All services must be carried out at an **authorised service centre**
- Oil changes must be **documented in the service booklet**
- The customer must **retain all service receipts** and make them available to AutoLease upon request

## Consequences of Poor Maintenance

Vehicle damage attributable to a failure to maintain the vehicle properly will be assessed at the time of return. The customer will be charged for any such damage over and above normal wear and tear.
