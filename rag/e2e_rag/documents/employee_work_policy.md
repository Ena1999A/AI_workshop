## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for a maximum of **2 days per week**. Remote work days must be agreed with the direct supervisor at the start of each month.

Remote employees must be reachable via Microsoft Teams during core hours: **9:00 – 15:00**.

Roles that require physical presence (vehicle inspection, reception, cashier) are not eligible for remote work.

## Annual Leave

| Employee Category | Annual Leave Entitlement |
|------------------|--------------------------|
| All full-time employees | 20 days |
| Employees with 5+ years of service | 22 days |
| Senior managers | 25 days |

Leave must be requested at least **7 days in advance**, except in emergencies. A maximum of **5 unused days** may be carried over to the following calendar year.

## Performance Reviews

Annual performance reviews are conducted each **December**. The review is based on:

- KPIs agreed at the start of the year
- Peer feedback
- Direct supervisor assessment

Employees receive a written review summary within **10 business days** of the review.

Performance scores run from 1 (unsatisfactory) to 5 (outstanding). Scores of 1 or 2 trigger a **performance improvement plan (PIP)** with a 90-day follow-up review.

## Salary Adjustments

Salaries are reviewed each **January** based on the previous December performance score:

| Score | Adjustment |
|-------|-----------|
| 1–2 | No adjustment (PIP period) |
| 3 | Cost-of-living increase (Croatian official inflation rate) |
| 4 | Cost-of-living increase + 3% |
| 5 | Cost-of-living increase + 5–8% (management discretion) |

## Working Hours and Breaks

The daily lunch break is **30 minutes** and is not counted as working time. Break times are agreed within each department based on shift and team structure.

Overtime is permitted only with written approval from a supervisor. It is compensated either with equivalent time off or financial payment in accordance with Croatian labour law.

## Dress Code

| Setting | Dress Code |
|---------|-----------|
| Standard office days | Business casual |
| Fridays | Casual permitted |
| Client-facing meetings (showroom or external) | Business attire required |
