## Monthly Installments

Monthly installments are due on the **5th of each month**. Each installment includes the principal repayment, interest, and insurance component.

Payments received after the 5th are subject to a late fee of **0.05% of the installment amount per day of delay**.

## Late Payment Stages

| Days Overdue | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| 15+ days | Delinquency may be reported to HROK |
| 30 days | Written warning issued |
| 60 days | Account flagged as delinquent |
| 90 days | AutoLease reserves the right to repossess the vehicle |

Repossession at 90 days does not require further notice beyond the warnings issued at 30 and 60 days.

## Payment Deferrals

Customers who anticipate difficulty meeting a payment deadline are encouraged to contact their account manager **at least 5 business days in advance**.

- One deferral per 12-month period may be granted without penalty.
- More than two deferrals within any 36-month period will trigger a mandatory contract review, which may result in revised terms or early termination.
- Deferral approval is at the sole discretion of the risk management team and is not guaranteed.

## Complaints and Escalation

Customers who wish to dispute a late fee or delinquency classification may file a formal complaint. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (HANFA).
