## Financial Leasing – Interest Rates

Annual interest rates for financial leasing range from **3.2% to 6.8%**, depending on the following factors:

- Customer creditworthiness
- Contract duration
- Vehicle type

**Electric vehicles** qualify for a preferential rate of **2.9% per annum**.

Rates are reviewed quarterly and may change for new contracts. The rate agreed at signing is fixed for the duration of that contract.

## Operational Leasing – Interest Rates

Operational leasing rates are fixed:

| Vehicle Category | Annual Rate |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Standard vehicles | 4.5% |
| Commercial vehicles | 5.1% |

## Returning vs. New Customers

The **3.9% fixed promotional rate** is available exclusively to returning customers who have successfully completed at least one prior leasing contract with AutoLease d.o.o. without incident.

New customers are subject to a variable rate starting at **5.5% per annum**, adjusted quarterly in line with EURIBOR movements. Customers are notified of rate changes at least 30 days in advance.
