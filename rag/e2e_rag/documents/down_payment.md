## Current Policy (effective March 2024)

A minimum down payment of **15% of the vehicle value** is required for all new leasing contracts. This applies to all vehicle categories, including vehicles valued under €30,000.

Customers may make a voluntary down payment of up to **30%** of the vehicle value to reduce their monthly installments.

## Previous Quotes

Customers who received a quote before March 2024 that did not include a mandatory down payment should contact their assigned agent to receive a revised offer reflecting the current policy.

---

> **Note for internal staff:** The pre-March 2024 policy stated no down payment was required for vehicles under €30,000. That version is no longer valid. If a customer references an older quote, acknowledge the change and issue a revised proposal.
