## Early Termination Fee

Customers may terminate their leasing contract before the scheduled end date. An early termination fee applies based on the number of remaining installments:

| Remaining Installments | Termination Fee |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Fewer than 12 | 3% of the outstanding principal balance |
| 12 or more | 5% of the outstanding principal balance |

## Notice Period

The customer must submit a written termination notice **at least 30 days in advance**. Verbal notices are not accepted.

## Vehicle Return

Following confirmed termination, AutoLease d.o.o. arranges vehicle collection. The cost of collection is borne by the customer.

The vehicle must be returned in good condition, accounting for normal wear and tear. Damage beyond normal use will be assessed and charged separately.
