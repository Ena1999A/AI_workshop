## Financial Leasing

In financial leasing, the customer assumes all economic rights and obligations related to the vehicle from the moment of handover. The vehicle appears on the customer's balance sheet. The customer is responsible for insurance, maintenance, and all costs associated with the vehicle. Ownership transfers to the customer at the end of the contract upon final payment or buyout.

## Operational Leasing

In operational leasing, the vehicle remains the property of AutoLease d.o.o. for the entire duration of the contract. The customer pays a fixed monthly fee for the right to use the vehicle and returns it at the end of the contract period. Operational leasing rates are fixed and do not fluctuate with market conditions.

## Sale-and-Leaseback

Sale-and-leaseback is a product available to business customers. A company sells a vehicle it already owns to AutoLease d.o.o. and simultaneously signs a leasing agreement for the same vehicle. This allows the company to release capital tied up in assets while retaining use of the vehicle.
