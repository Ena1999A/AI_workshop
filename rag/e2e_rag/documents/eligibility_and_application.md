## Application Channels

Applications may be submitted:

- **Online** — processed within 24 hours
- **In branch** — processed within 3 business days

## Credit Assessment

All applications are subject to a credit check conducted via HROK and AutoLease internal databases.

- **Standard credit check** — applies to all applicants
- **Extended credit check** — applies to business (legal entity) applicants; includes a review of business financials

## Private Individuals – Eligibility Requirements

- Proof of stable income
- Demonstrated creditworthiness
- Croatian residency
- Minimum 6 months of continuous employment

Students and retirees may apply with a **co-applicant (guarantor)** who independently meets the standard eligibility criteria.

## Business (Legal Entity) – Eligibility Requirements

Required documentation:

- Excerpt from the court register
- Financial statements for the last 2 years
- Tax clearance certificate (potvrda Porezne uprave o nepostojanju duga)
- Completed leasing application form

Business customers with 5 or more vehicles may qualify for **fleet leasing with volume discounts**.

## Tax Considerations for Business Customers

- VAT is charged on monthly installments and may be reclaimed as input VAT
- Operational leasing installments are recognised as a business expense and are tax-deductible
