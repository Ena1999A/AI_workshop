## Overview

At the end of a leasing contract, customers have three options:

1. **Purchase the vehicle** at the pre-agreed residual value
2. **Return the vehicle** to AutoLease d.o.o.
3. **Extend the contract** for an additional 12-month period

## Option 1 – Vehicle Purchase

The residual (buyout) value is fixed at the time of contract signing and does not change based on market conditions. It is not subject to renegotiation.

Disputes regarding residual value must be submitted **in writing within 14 days of the contract end date**.

## Option 2 – Vehicle Return

Returned vehicles undergo an inspection by an authorised assessor. The inspection report is delivered to the customer within **5 business days**. Any damage beyond normal wear and tear will be charged to the customer.

## Option 3 – Contract Extension

A 12-month extension is available upon request. Extension terms, including the interest rate and monthly installment, may differ from the original contract and will reflect current market conditions.

*To notify AutoLease of your end-of-contract choice, contact your account manager at least 30 days before the contract end date.*
