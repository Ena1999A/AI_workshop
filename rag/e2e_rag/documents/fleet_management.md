## Fleet Overview

AutoLease d.o.o. maintains an internal fleet of **12 vehicles** for business use. Permitted uses include:

- Client visits
- Vehicle transport
- Management travel

Fleet vehicles are assigned by the **operations manager** based on availability and the nature of the trip. Personal use of fleet vehicles is not permitted.

## Vehicle Access

To access a fleet vehicle, employees must have a **signed fleet agreement** on file with the operations department. Contact the operations department at **ext. 101** to arrange access or report any issues.

## Maintenance Schedule

All fleet vehicles must be serviced every **15,000 km or 12 months**, whichever comes first.

Service records must be submitted to the operations manager within **5 business days** of the service being completed.

## Fuel and Cost Reimbursement

Fuel costs are reimbursed monthly. Employees must submit fuel receipts to the operations manager by the **last working day of each month**.
