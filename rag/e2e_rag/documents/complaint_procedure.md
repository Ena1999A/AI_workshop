## Intake

All customer complaints must be received via the official complaint form available on the internal portal under:

> **Customers → Complaints → New Entry**

The form is completed by the customer service agent. Customers do not fill in the form themselves.

## Processing Timelines

| Stage | Responsible | Deadline |
|-------|------------|----------|
| Initial review | Assigned agent | 3 business days from receipt |
| Escalation decision | Agent | End of initial review period |
| Escalated case resolution | Department head | 10 business days from escalation |
| Director escalation | Director | Automatic if head misses deadline |

Cases requiring review of contract documentation are escalated to the department head immediately after initial review.

If the department head does not issue a decision within 10 business days, the case automatically escalates to director level.

## Documentation

All complaint-related documentation must be archived in the CRM under the customer's profile. Physical documents are scanned and attached to the digital file. Original paper documents are stored in the document archive (room 204).

## Customer Communication

All responses to customers are sent by email. Telephone communication alone is not sufficient — any phone resolution must be followed by a written email confirmation.

Communication with customers is conducted in Croatian by default. English may be used upon the customer's request, with the approval of the team lead.

## Escalation to HANFA

If a complaint remains unresolved after the internal process, the customer has the right to escalate to the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (HANFA). Staff should inform customers of this right when closing an unresolved case.
