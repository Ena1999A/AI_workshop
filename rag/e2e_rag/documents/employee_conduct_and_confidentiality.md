## Scope

These rules apply to all AutoLease d.o.o. employees and came into force on 1 January 2023. All employees are required to read this document and sign an acknowledgement form, which is retained in their personnel file.

Violations may result in disciplinary proceedings, temporary suspension, or termination of employment, depending on severity.

## Customer Data and Confidentiality

- Customer personal data must not be shared with third parties without the customer's written consent and approval from the legal department.
- Employees with CRM access are responsible for the accuracy of all data they enter and update.
- Any data entry error must be reported to a supervisor immediately.

## Credentials and System Access

- Login credentials must never be shared with colleagues, even in an emergency.
- Each employee has a personal user account and is individually responsible for all actions taken under that account.

## Business Confidentiality

The following are classified as business secrets and must not be disclosed externally:

- Service pricing and customer-specific contract terms
- Internal bonus and commission structures
- Software tools, systems, and internal processes
- Customer personal data
- Any internal customer assessment or risk methodology

## Gifts and Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must not accept gifts from clients or suppliers worth more than **€30**.
- Any conflict of interest — where a personal interest could influence a business decision — must be reported to a supervisor immediately upon becoming aware of it.

## Use of Company Resources

Company resources (printer, company vehicles, telephone, internet connection) must not be used for personal purposes. Misuse of company resources is treated as misappropriation of company property.

## Handling Customer Complaints

- Any customer complaint received verbally must be recorded in writing and entered into the CRM on the same day.
- All complaints must be forwarded to the customer service department on the day they are received.

## Offboarding

Employees leaving the company must, on their final working day:

- Return all company property (phone, laptop, office access card)
- Hand over all business documentation and access credentials to HR
- Sign a declaration of asset return and non-disclosure of business secrets
