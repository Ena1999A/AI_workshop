## Scope

This policy applies to all vehicle leasing contracts issued by AutoLease d.o.o. on or after 1 January 2020. It covers both private individuals and legal entities operating in the Republic of Croatia.

Where individual contract terms conflict with this policy, the individual contract prevails — except where this policy reflects mandatory provisions of Croatian consumer protection law, in which case this policy takes precedence.

## Contract Duration

Leasing contracts run for a minimum of **12 months** and a maximum of **84 months**.

## Vehicle Ownership

AutoLease d.o.o. retains legal ownership of the vehicle for the full duration of the contract. The customer has the right to use the vehicle in accordance with the contract terms.

## Data Retention

Personal data collected during the contract period is retained for **7 years** in accordance with Croatian law. Customers may opt out of marketing communications at any time by submitting a written request.

## Complaints

Customers may submit complaints to AutoLease d.o.o. directly. All complaints are processed within **30 business days**. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (**HANFA**).

## Indirect Liability

AutoLease d.o.o. is not liable for indirect damages arising from vehicle defects or failures by third-party suppliers (e.g. manufacturers, insurers).
